RETURN & REFUND POLICY

BRAND: YOUNG & STUPID • LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 8, 2026
MADE-TO-ORDER PRODUCTION

At YOUNG & STUPID, our products are made to order. Because each item is produced specifically for the customer after an order is placed, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges for incorrect size selections, incorrect color selections, accidental orders, or change of mind.

01 RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

We generally do not accept returns or exchanges for:

  • Wrong size selected by the customer
  • Wrong color selected by the customer
  • Change of mind
  • Accidental orders
  • Customer no longer wanting the product

Customers should carefully review the product details and size guide before placing an order.

02 DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE OR INCORRECT PRODUCTS

If your order arrives:

  • Damaged
  • Defective
  • Misprinted
  • Incorrectly fulfilled
  • With the wrong product

You may be eligible for a replacement or refund.

Please contact us as soon as possible after receiving your order.

To process a claim, we may require:
  • Order number
  • Customer name
  • Description of the issue
  • Clear photos of the product
  • Photos showing the defect, damage, or printing issue

03 LOST ORDERS

If your package is lost during delivery, contact us with your order number.

We will investigate the shipment and provide the appropriate resolution according to the applicable fulfillment and shipping policy.

04 REFUNDS

If a refund is approved, it will normally be issued to the original payment method used for the order.

Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment provider, bank, or card issuer.

All store prices and refunds are processed in USD ($).

05 RETURNED OR UNDELIVERABLE PACKAGES

Additional shipping or reshipment charges may apply if an order is returned because of:

  • Incorrect shipping address
  • Failed delivery
  • Unclaimed package
  • Customer-related delivery issues
  • Customs-related issues

06 INTERNATIONAL ORDERS

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or other charges imposed by the destination country.

Any applicable charges that are legally payable by the recipient are the customer's responsibility unless otherwise stated at checkout.

07 LEGAL CONSUMER RIGHTS

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

Where mandatory consumer protection laws provide additional rights, those rights will apply.

08 HOW TO CONTACT US

For return, refund, damage, defect, or order issues, contact:

YOUNG & STUPID
Email: support@youngandstupid.com

Please include your order number when contacting customer support.

09 POLICY CHANGES

YOUNG & STUPID may update this Return & Refund Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
